# Indonesia ordinary-series expansion — 3 November 2026

Adds 19 designs (38 images), bringing the gallery to 278 designs and 556 images.

- Seven TE2016 National Heroes designs, introduced 19 December 2016.
- Eight earlier designs: TE2000 Rp1,000; TE2001 Rp5,000; TE2004 Rp20,000/Rp100,000; TE2005 Rp10,000/Rp50,000; TE2009 Rp2,000; TE2010 purple Rp10,000 security revision.
- Four TE1998/1999 designs: Rp10,000, Rp20,000, Rp50,000 and polymer Rp100,000.

## Evidence and dates

[Bank Indonesia's catalogue](https://www.bi.go.id/en/rupiah/gambar-uang/default.aspx) lists the surviving earlier issues. Its [TE2016 Rp1,000 detail](https://www.bi.go.id/en/rupiah/gambar-uang/Detail-Uang.aspx?Bahan=Kertas&ID=17) confirms 19 December 2016, cotton fibre and 141 × 65 mm. TE2016 detail IDs 11–17 cover the seven denominations.

[Bank Indonesia's withdrawal announcement](https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_611008.aspx) withdraws the four TE1998/1999 notes effective 31 December 2008. Their inclusive circulation end is 30 December 2008; later redemption eligibility is not circulation.

Individual issue dates, dimensions and design revisions are cross-referenced against the [historical banknote table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banknotes_of_the_Indonesian_rupiah). Where an exact introduction was not independently established, the record retains year precision. The 2010 purple Rp10,000 is separate from the 2005 magenta design. Signature and serial changes are not separate records.

The [2022 government introduction notice](https://setkab.go.id/en/govt-central-bank-issue-2022-rupiah-banknotes) explicitly preserves earlier notes' legal-tender status until withdrawal. Replacement dates are therefore not used as circulation ends. Earlier designs are labelled “Not issued”, distinct from “Withdrawn”.

## Images

Per-record Banknote Museum pages identify each image pair. All supplied markings are retained. New images are reduced to at most 72 dpi at original dimensions and overprinted SPECIMEN. Article 24(1) of [Law 7/2011 on Currency (English translation)](https://www.flevin.com/id/lgso/translations/JICA%20Mirror/english/4645_UU_7_2011_e.html) provides an educational/promotional exception for imitations bearing “specimen”. This treatment does not establish separate source copyright permission, which remains unresolved; these are attributed educational references, not a claim of blanket rights clearance.

## Deferred work

Indonesia's 2011/2014 security revisions and pre-1998 issues still need review. China's fourth and earlier fifth series have been researched but are not imported in this batch: the [2019 image-use rules](https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/flfggz/flfggzbmgz/202101/t20210105_146395.html) have approval and single-sided-use conditions; low resolution alone does not establish a universal approval exemption. Existing China images have not been reclassified as cleared.
