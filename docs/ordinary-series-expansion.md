# Ordinary-series expansion — 3 November 2026

First implementation batch: 77 designs, bringing the catalogue to 259 notes / 518 images.

- Hong Kong: 41 additions. All three issuers' 2003 and 2010 series; HSBC and Standard Chartered 2018 series; government paper $10.
- Macau: 18 additions. Current BNU series, BNU 2005/2009 series and BOC 2008 edition.
- Euro: six first-series notes, using the ECB's downloadable 72 dpi SPECIMEN archive.
- Switzerland: sixth and eighth series, six denominations each. SNB images are reduced to no more than 72 dpi at original dimensions and marked SPECIMEN. Existing markings are never removed.

The reviewed manifest is `scripts/data/ordinary-series.json`; `scripts/add-ordinary-series.py` adds only missing IDs. Existing images are checked before reuse. The importer validates both sides before writing the updated catalogue and can resume an interrupted download. Signature-only variants are not included.

## Date evidence

- [HKMA 2003 introduction](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2003/11/20031130-3): $100/$500, 15 December 2003.
- [HKMA 2004 introduction](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2004/10/20041010-3): $20/$50/$1000, 11 October 2004.
- [HKMA 2010/2011 introduction](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2011/02/20110215-7): $1000, 7 December 2010; $500, 22 February 2011.
- [HKMA November 2011 introduction](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2011/11/20111115-5): $50 for all issuers and $100 for BOC/SCB, 22 November 2011. HSBC $100 is recorded at 2012 precision.
- [HKMA 2012 report](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/doc/key-information/press-release/2012/20120608e3a1.pdf): $20, 11 January 2012.
- [BNU issuing history](https://www.bnu.com.mo/en/our-bank/bank-note-issuing/bank-note-issuing): current six denominations introduced in pairs on 1 February 2024, 20 January 2025 and 6 February 2026; earlier issue 8 August 2005, $50 8 August 2009.
- [BOC circulating editions](https://www.bankofchina.com/mo/en/aboutus/ab4/ab41/): 2008 edition is still circulating; [contemporary issue report](https://banknotenews.com?p=17141) places public introduction in 2009, distinct from its printed date. Recorded at year precision.
- [ECB current series](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/banknotes/current/html/index.en.html): first series introduced 1 January 2002, still legal tender.
- [SNB all series](https://www.snb.ch/en/the-snb/mandates-goals/cash/all-series): individual introductions and recalls. In particular eighth-series CHF10 = 8 April 1997 and CHF20 = 1 October 1996; these supersede erroneous secondary-table dates. Inclusive ends are 30 April 2000 and 29 April 2021.

SAR image pairs and motif descriptions are attributed per record to Banknote Museum. Availability is not blanket copyright clearance. HKMA exempts screen-only digital reproductions from its physical-reproduction approval, but separately directs users to issuer copyright consent; that consent has not been obtained. Macau image reproduction/source rights remain unresolved. These limitations apply to the previously bundled SAR images as well as this batch; no watermark removal or permission claim is made.

## Remaining ordinary-series backlog

This batch starts the ordinary-series work; it is not exhaustive. Remaining work includes older SAR designs, China, India, Mexico, Indonesia, Poland, Israel, Sweden, Korea, earlier US designs, Canadian additions, Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Russia and Argentina. UK regional issuers and pre-euro currencies remain separate larger inventories. No new commemoratives or collector-only issues were added.
