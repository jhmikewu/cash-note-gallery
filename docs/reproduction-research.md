# Image reproduction research — 3 November 2026

This is a record of source checks, not a blanket legal clearance. Availability on an issuer or museum website does not by itself grant reuse rights. Existing source markings must never be removed. A replacement needs both reproduction-rule and source-rights checks.

| Collection | Source | Finding for this flat digital gallery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK | https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/banknotes/using-images-of-banknotes | Digital reproductions showing more than half a note require SPECIMEN unless the prescribed slant exception is met. Applies to withdrawn notes too. New UK variants preserve issuer-supplied markings. |
| Euro | https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/banknotes/images/html/index.en.html | Full digital images require diagonal SPECIMEN and resolution no greater than 72 dpi at original size. Retain markings. |
| Canada | https://www.bankofcanada.ca/banknotes/using-bank-note-images/ | Flat images showing more than half a note require SPECIMEN. Unmarked museum scans are not sufficient evidence of an exception. Commercial use requires written permission. |
| Australia | https://banknotes.rba.gov.au/legal/reproducing-banknotes/ | Electronic images have size and resolution conditions, including less than three-quarters dimensions at 100% and no more than 72 dpi. An unmarked source does not authorize unrestricted resolution. |
| Switzerland | https://www.snb.ch/dam/jcr:7ab35b8a-2bd5-4ad7-8255-52711da841ac/instr_rep_notes.en.pdf | Public electronic images require SPECIMEN and no more than 72 dpi. Historical sixth/eighth series are not covered by the exemption for first–fifth/seventh series. |
| Japan | https://www.mof.go.jp/faq/currency/07af.htm | Ministry of Finance distinguishes publishing photographed images online from producing potentially confusing printed imitations. Source-image rights remain separate. BOJ website images require prior contact: https://www.boj.or.jp/en/note_tfjgs/note/security/gizo0410a.htm. |
| US | https://www.uscurrency.gov/media/currency-image-use | No universal SPECIMEN condition; size, one-sided reproduction, and production-material conditions apply. |

Other issuers still need individual review. No existing marked image has been replaced on the assumption that a museum or Wikimedia copy automatically clears all conditions.
